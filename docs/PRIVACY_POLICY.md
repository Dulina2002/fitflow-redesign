# FitFlow Mobile Application - Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: October 2026**

FitFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal information and privacy rights. This policy outlines our collection, storage, and processing practices.

### 1. Data We Collect
* **Account Information:** Email address, username, profile photo.
* **Health & Fitness Metrics:** Workout logs, target heart rate, calorie burn estimates, and body weight.
* **Camera & Visual Data:** Photos captured for meal nutrition scanning. Images are processed ephemerally using computer vision and are never sold to third parties.

### 2. Permissions Justification
* **Camera (android.permission.CAMERA):** Required exclusively for capturing food items to log nutrition automatically.
* **Storage / Media Access:** Used to upload user profile pictures and save completed workout summary cards.
* **Health Connect / Apple HealthKit:** Accessed solely to sync daily active energy and physical steps.

### 3. Data Protection and User Rights
All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and databases are protected using AES-256 encryption. Users reserve the explicit right to export their data or permanently request account and data deletion via support@fitflow.app.
